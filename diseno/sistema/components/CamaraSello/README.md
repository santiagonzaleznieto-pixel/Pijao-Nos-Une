# CamaraSello

In-app camera viewfinder with a seal: the façade photo, the problem photos and the paper authorization (24.1, step 2 of the visit).

- Frame guide with rounded corners and a short hint at the top («Encuadre toda la fachada»).
- **Seal** across the bottom, on a translucent dark band: campaign name, date and time, coordinates and GPS precision. It is burned into the photo, so it can't be edited afterwards.
- Shutter button: 76 px, navy ring and orange center (the visit's main action); it sinks on press.
- «Sin foto» registers that the person didn't authorize the photo; it never skips the step by mistake.
- **What the consumer provides:** the photo type, the GPS reading for the seal and where it is saved (the encrypted queue, never the phone's gallery).
- The viewfinder is always dark, whatever the mode, so the scene reads clearly; the light text sits on a dark band (more than 7:1).
